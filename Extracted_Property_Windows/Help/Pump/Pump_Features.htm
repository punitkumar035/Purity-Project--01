Pump A pump station is used to increase the pressure of a flow stream.

 

 

 

 

General Features  Pump stations are used to increase the pressure of a flow stream to prevent juice flashing in heat exchangers, to raise the pressure of condensate into a flash tank, or to condense vapor in a flow. Water/vapor phase changes can occur in a pump station. For example, vapor in a liquid flow stream can condense when the pressure of a flow stream is increase by a pump.
