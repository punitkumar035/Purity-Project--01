About Station Modules

 

The following station modules are used to construct a flow diagram of the process being evaluated. Shapes for each of these stations are provided on the Sugars stencils.

NAME

 

DESCRIPTION

 

Blender

Another flow stream is blended into a primary flow stream by ratio, or to a specified value in the output flow.

Centrifugal

Massecuite is separated into green (molasses) and sugar (2-Output), or green, wash and sugar (3-Output) output flows.

Compressor

Flow stream pressure and temperature are increased.

Contact Condenser

Vapor is condensed using direct contact with cold water.

Cooler

Flow stream is cooled by a specified amount.

Crystallizer

Flow stream is cooled and sucrose crystals are grown.

Distributor

Input flow is split into output flows (up to 10 in quantity).

Dryer

Flow stream is dried using coil heating.

Evaporator

Water is evaporated from a flow stream.

Flash Tank

Flow stream is cooled by vapor flash.

Heat Exchanger

Flow stream is heated by coil heat exchange; for example, tube and shell or plate heat exchangers.

Injection Heater

Flow stream is heated by direct injection of heating flow into the flow stream.

Melter

Combines up to ten input process flows and dissolves sugar crystals. Also, the output flow can be controlled to a specified % dry substance by adjusting one of the input flows.

Pan

Grows sugar crystals while evaporating water.

Pressure Reducer

Pressure of flow stream is reduced.

Pump

Pressure of flow stream is increased.

Reactor

Components may be reacted in a flow stream to produce other components and solubility coefficients may be specified.

Receiver

Up to ten input flows are combined into one output flow.

Separator/Filter

Flow stream is separated into two output flow streams having different component fractions. A diluent flow may, or may not, be used to assist with the separation.

Surface Condenser

Vapor is condensed by non-contact heat transfer between the vapor and cold water.

Tank

Up to ten input process flows are combined into one output flow and material may be stored, or removed from storage with, or without heating. Also, the output flow can be controlled to a specified % dry substance by adjusting one of the input flows.

Thermocompressor

Pressure and temperature of a vapor flow stream are increased using high-pressure motive steam.

Turbine

Pressure and temperature of a steam flow are decreased and energy is removed.

Turbo Alternator

Electrical power is generated using a steam driven turbine.

 

Some stations have optional entries for more than one input flow stream, for example, melters, tanks, receivers, and separators/filters. Data that controls the performance of each station is entered using the respective station properties windows in Sugars.

 

Building a new model is simply a matter of dragging shapes from the Sugars stencils to the drawing surface, connecting the shapes with the Visio connector tool to reflect the process flows in the factory, connecting flows that leave the model to output ports on the appropriate stations, and adding all external flows that go into the model from outside sources.

 

The shapes in the Sugars stencils represent stations that are available in Sugars for building a model of the process. New shapes can be designed and named to have the same properties as any of the station modules available in Sugars (see Designing Your Own Shapes)

 

Also, station shapes can be grouped together and dragged from the stencil as a group. Commonly used arrangements of stations can be made into a group to speed the building of models. For example, one evaporator body can be grouped with receivers, distributors, and a flash tank to represent one complete effect with flashing in a multiple effect model. Some groups of shapes are supplied with the Sugars stencils, but other groupings can be created and added to the stencils (see Grouping Shapes).
